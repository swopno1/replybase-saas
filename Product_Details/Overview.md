# Product Overview: ReplyBase

## Core of the Application
ReplyBase is a **Multi-Channel Communication Orchestra** designed specifically for small businesses and individual service providers. It acts as the central hub (the "Orchestra") that synchronizes customer interactions across diverse platforms—including Webchat, Telegram, and (planned) TikTok—into a single, manageable stream.

The core power of ReplyBase lies in its flexibility: it can function as a streamlined, manual communication tool or be upgraded with an **AI "Brain"** (powered by Typebot or internal Flows) for intense, automated customer engagement.

## Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** To be the default communication operating system for every UK-based small business, transforming fragmented messages into meaningful business relationships.
- **Mission:** To empower small businesses with high-tier CRM capabilities and AI automation, simplified into a "miniature CRM" that requires zero technical expertise to manage.

## Core Problem Solved
Small businesses in the UK often lose leads because they are overwhelmed by messages across too many channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, Website, Social Media).
1. **Communication Fragmentation:** Leads are missed when business owners forget to check a specific app.
2. **Engagement Lag:** Customers expect instant replies; small business owners are busy and cannot always respond immediately.
3. **Lead Management Chaos:** Turning a chat message into a tracked "Lead" or "Contact" is usually a manual, error-prone process.

ReplyBase solves this by centralizing all messages, providing an optional AI to handle the "first touch" and qualification, and automatically organizing every conversation into a structured CRM.

## What ReplyBase Does
- **Aggregates Channels:** Connects Webchat widgets, Telegram bots, and social channels into one unified inbox.
- **Automates with AI:** Provides an optional, intense engagement layer using Typebot as a "brain" to handle complex queries and bookings.
- **Simplifies CRM:** Automatically captures contact details (name, email, phone) from conversations and tracks them through a lead pipeline.
- **Workspace Isolation:** Ensures complete data privacy for multi-tenant environments, making it suitable for agencies managing multiple clients.

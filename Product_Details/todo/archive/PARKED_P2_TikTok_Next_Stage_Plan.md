# P2: TikTok Next-Stage Plan

Status: parked  
Owner: Product + Engineering + Marketing  
Target window: deferred

## Decision

TikTok expansion is parked. It is not part of the active product cycle.

## Why Parked

Current strategic priority is unified inbox clarity and operational consistency for:

- Website chat
- Telegram
- Email intake (planned)
- Contact-form intake (planned)

## Unpark Gates

TikTok can be reconsidered only when all gates are met:

1. Source parity is stable across active and planned inbox sources.
2. Diagnostics model is unified and support-ready.
3. Production release gates are automated and consistently enforced.
4. Activation and retention KPIs are healthy after unified inbox rollout.

## Interim Action

Keep this document for traceability, but do not schedule implementation work until unpark gates are reviewed and approved.

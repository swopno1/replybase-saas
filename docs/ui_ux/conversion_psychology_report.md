# Conversion Psychology Report - ReplyBase (August 2026)

## 1. Trust & Authority
- **Visual Polish**: Professional UI reduces perceived risk for B2B users.
- **Transparency**: Clear plan limits and usage tracking prevent "billing surprises."

## 2. Friction Management
- **Onboarding**: AI-assisted setup creates an immediate sense of "Value Realization."
- **Trial Strategy**: 14-day trials are emphasized to lower the barrier to entry.

## 3. Psychological Hooks
- **Milestones**: Gamified progress tracking in the dashboard encourages users to reach "Setup Complete" status.
- **Real-time Gratification**: Streaming AI responses demonstrate product capability instantly.

## 4. Conversion Roadmap
- **Testimonials**: Strategic placement of customer success signals within the dashboard.
- **ROI Calculator**: Proposed interactive tool to show potential time-savings with ReplyBase automation.

# Conversion Psychology Review

## Core Principles Applied
- **Authority**: The AI demonstrates deep knowledge of "Omnichannel Continuity" and "CRM Synchronization".
- **Commitment & Consistency**: By asking small questions first (business type, volume), the system builds momentum towards the email capture (ROI projection).

2. **Confidence**: Built by the AI's ability to extract and confirm business data.
3. **Safety**: Ensured by the prominent "Talk to a Human" option.

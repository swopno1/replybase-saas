## Maintenance Rules

1. Do not add strategy or roadmap files in `extra`.
2. Keep long-lived product strategy in `Product_Details`.
3. Move one-off reports and completed summaries directly into `archive`.
4. Keep this index aligned to active docs only.
5. Keep `extra` limited to operational runbooks and deployment/security references.

## Current Product Snapshot

- Main dashboard navigation includes Dashboard, Bots, Contacts, Conversations, Leads, Flows, Facebook Pages, Telegram Bot, Webchat Widget, AI Status, Billing, Activity, and Settings.
- Current channel surfaces implemented in the app are Facebook Pages, Telegram Bot, and Webchat Widget.
- Webchat has the most complete embedded-channel documentation and test coverage.
- Billing is active through Stripe checkout and the customer portal.
- Some areas are still MVP-level, especially Activity and parts of Settings and usage analytics.
